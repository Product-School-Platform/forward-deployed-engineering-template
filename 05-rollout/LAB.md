The three rungs change who uses the draft. They do not add a tool.

- Shadow: the draft sits beside Priya. She works as before. You compare its blockers with what Elena later returns.

- Supervised: empty blockers, or every blocker named to a person, on Dana's slice of 15. Priya still signs the send. There is no fourth rung in this engagement.
