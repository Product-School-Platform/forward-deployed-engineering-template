Walk the learner one step at a time. Stop and ask. Do not promote Harbor because the Module 4 trace was clean.

1. Ask which rung Harbor is on today, and what evidence in the engagement or the desk sample supports it.
2. Ask for the gate to the next rung, in Dana's numbers or Elena's returns.
3. Ask what that next rung does not unlock. If they grant `memo.submit`, take it back.
4. Ask for the incident path: who turns the draft off the same day, what the relationship manager does that hour, and when the sponsor is told.
5. If the model is the subject of the off switch, have them rewrite the sentence.
6. Write their answers into `05-rollout/rollout-ladder.md`.
7. They commit. Message: `Place Harbor on a rung.`
