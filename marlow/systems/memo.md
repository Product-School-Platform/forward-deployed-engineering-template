# Memo

Memo is the only paper the credit committee reads. Ledger, Atlas, Vault, and Screen are inputs.

## What a committee pack contains

1. Facility history, with the Ledger figures and the waiver status.
2. Relationship, from Atlas, including the legal entity as Atlas spells it.
3. Document checklist: what is in the right Vault folder, what is missing.
4. Screen status, quoted, not interpreted into a clear.
5. Use of proceeds, including any related party above the policy line.
6. The recommendation. Priya writes this. The draft may leave it blank.

## What Memo is not

- It is not the email to Elena.
- It is not the committee invitation.
- It is not the minute. Ruth Pell writes the minute after the sitting, into `Memo/CL-7781/minute/`. That folder is empty.

A staging draft lives at `Memo/CL-7781/draft/` and nowhere else. Creating it is `memo.draft`. Sending it is not a tool.
