# Marlow: commercial client onboarding

This folder is the engagement. Read it in order. Do not skip ahead to a model.

You are embedded at Marlow, a fictional regional bank, for one engagement. The desk onboards commercial credit facilities. A relationship manager builds a file from five systems. Compliance reviews it. The credit committee reads one memo. The client is waiting.

The five systems:

| System | What it holds | Who may write |
|---|---|---|
| Ledger | Facilities, balances, covenants | Onboarding operations, after a committee minute exists in Memo |
| Atlas | The relationship: legal entity, owner, meetings | Relationship data team |
| Vault | Documents | The relationship manager files them. The folder is often wrong. |
| Screen | Sanctions and KYC status | Northline, a vendor. Marlow does not own this system. |
| Memo | The paper the committee reads | The relationship manager drafts. The committee secretary records the minute. |

Start with `engagement.md`, then `cast.md`, then `shadow/priya-week.md`. The systems and the policy extracts are the record. Priya's notes are not.

The pilot tools, and the only tools, are listed in `tools.md`.

Today in the case is Friday 16 October 2026.
