# Credit policy extract

Applies to commercial facility increases. The rest of the policy is out of scope for this engagement.

1. Interest coverage below 2.0x blocks an increase unless a waiver for that breach is filed on the facility, under the legal entity Atlas names.
2. If a subsidiary is more than 20% of revenue, its financials have to be in Vault before the memo goes to committee.
3. Use of proceeds paid to a related party above $250,000 is disclosed in its own section of the memo, with the person, the company, and the amount. A related party includes a director who owns 30% or more of the payee.
4. The relationship manager signs the memo that goes to committee. A draft is not a signature.
5. Ledger is updated only after the committee minute is in Memo.

A memo that misses 1, 2, or 3 is a compliance return. Elena sends it back. She does not fix the folder.
