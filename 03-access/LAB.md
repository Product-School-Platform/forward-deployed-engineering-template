# Module 3 lab

Walk the learner one step at a time. Stop and ask. Do not add a tool.

Do not use em dashes or en dashes in anything you write. Use commas, colons, or full stops.

1. Open `marlow/tools.md` and their cut in `02-workflow/workflow-cut.md`.
2. Ask the four questions: untrusted content, actions and whose identity, blast radius, observability.
3. The actor on the draft is `svc-onboarding-draft`. Ask who is accountable for what leaves staging.
4. If they add `screen.clear`, `ledger.book`, or `memo.submit`, refuse it and show the tool list.
5. Ask who signs Screen and the policy holes, who signs the off switch, and who signs the spend. Those are three different decisions. If they name a signer and cannot point at a line in the cast or the letter, send them back. A job title from memory is not a citation. Do not hand them the names.
6. Write their answers into `03-access/access-boundary.md`.
7. They commit. Message: `Write the access boundary.`
