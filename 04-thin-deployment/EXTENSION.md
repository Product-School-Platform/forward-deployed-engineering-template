# Optional extension

Between sessions. Not graded. About 45 to 60 minutes. Skip it if you have no system you are allowed to touch.

Do not use em dashes or en dashes in anything you write. Use commas, colons, or full stops.

Marlow's one Harbor trace is the submission. This page does not add a sixth Marlow tool and does not change `04-thin-deployment/thin-deployment.md`.

1. Pick one read-only lookup on a system you can actually reach. Same shape as the adapter: one call, one log line, one refusal you can show.
2. Bring back one trace and one sentence on what the product should absorb.
3. Do not put customer data in `marlow-dossier`. Keep this in the second private repo if you have one, or in notes that stay off the course repo.

Stop and ask before you call anything that can send, book, or clear.
